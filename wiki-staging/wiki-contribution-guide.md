# Wiki Contribution Guide

Anyone with a hack.tez domain can propose and edit articles. Moderators review changes and enforce basic content standards.

## Workflow
1. Propose a new article or edit an existing one.
2. Include a concise summary and relevant tags.
3. Link to primary sources where possible.

## Standards
- Keep content neutral and verifiable.
- Avoid shilling or non-factual claims.
- Prefer canonical docs over third-party blogs.

## Moderation
Moderators can lock or archive pages in case of disputes or abuse. Persistent abuse may result in a soft ban.
