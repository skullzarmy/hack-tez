Paint with Paraxeno: questions and answers

Paintings that paint themselves, from my own art sets. They move too: the generator makes square looping GIFs and videos. The menus sit in a menu bar under the title. Open a question to read the answer.

Getting started

What is this?

A picture generator. It paints a picture by itself from my own artwork (the photo sets), then adds glitch, symmetry, colour print, glow, texture and frames on top. You choose as much or as little as you like and the seed decides the rest.

What does the seed do?

The seed is the picture's number. The same seed with the same menus gives the same picture. New picture picks a new seed. Any menu left on "seed decides" is chosen by that seed.

What is the difference between New picture, New glitch and Surprise me?
  • New picture: a new seed and a fresh painting.
  • New glitch: the same painting, with the glitch and finishing effects re-rolled.
  • Surprise me: randomizes every menu and picks a new seed.

New glitch is greyed out when no glitch or effect is turned on, because it would have nothing to change.

What does "Lock this look" do?

It keeps your settings, and any random choices already made, and gives you a new seed. Good for "same style, different picture".

To put the same glitch on another base: tick Lock, then change the base (add your own image and use it exactly with Menus on my image set to yes, or change the source or palette) and press New picture. With Lock ticked, Surprise me still randomises the painting and finishing menus but keeps everything in Glitch (glitch styles, amount, pixelate, Signal noise) as you set it. The glitch pattern itself gets a new seed.

How long does a picture take?

A few seconds. The "Painting..." label shows while it works. Big GIFs take longer, see below.

How big is the picture? Where are the settings?

In this version the picture fills the view, as big as the window allows. The settings sit in a panel beside it (or over it on a phone). Press Close to hide the panel and see the picture bigger, and press Settings to bring it back. The View box with A- and A+ makes the text in the panel smaller or bigger.

What is in the saved parameters text?

The first lines say which build made it (for example V6_73), then the seed, the glitch seed and every menu with its value. Apply puts them back. Files saved with older versions (they say V4 on the first line) still load.

Where are the menus and buttons?

The menu bar sits under the title: Start, Adjust, Look, Glitch, Touches, Pareidolia and Layers. Press a name to open it, then press an item to see its choices. The small grey text under an item shows what it is set to, or why it is greyed out. The pop-ups are made to stay on the screen, also in a narrow frame like Google Sites.

  • Start: the seed, Pictures from, your own images, Use my image exactly, Menus on my image.
  • Adjust: Comic look and simple corrections.
  • Look: palette, plates, elements, brush, layout, collage style, background, rarity, metal.
  • Glitch: glitch styles, amount, Signal noise, Block stretch, Pixel stretch, Colour pass.
  • Touches: symmetry, colour print, light, texture, plaid, frames, Colorama.
  • Pareidolia: faces.

Under the picture: New picture, New glitch, Surprise me, Save PNG, Keep this look; then Previous IMG, Next IMG, Undo, Redo, Clear steps, Restore last look; then the ticks and the red dashed buttons Clear parameters and Clear kept. A button is greyed out when it has nothing to do.

What do Undo, Redo, Clear steps and Restore last look do?

Every time you change the look, the page remembers it (up to 40 steps, kept only while the page is open). Undo goes back one step, Redo puts it back, Clear steps forgets them all.

Restore last look brings back the look you had the last time you used the page, even after closing it. It uses your browser's storage, so it only works if your browser allows that; if not, it tells you there is nothing saved. Note: a collage layout picks its pictures again each time, so an undone or restored collage can show other pictures from your set.

What are Layers? Can I make my own collage?

Yes. Open Layers in the menu bar and press Add overlay (up to 8). Each layer is one of your own images (add them first in Start) and has its own controls: Blend (normal, additive, multiply, screen, soft light, difference), Applied (before or after the glitches), Opacity, Move left-right, Move up-down, Size across and Size down, Angle (0 to 360), Repeat (tiles), Keep its shape and Reset this layer. The layer tick turns one layer on or off, and Up and Down change the order.

Press Blend layers to turn the result into one picture; New picture, New glitch, Variations and the Adjust menus then work on it. Undo this brings the layers back. Layers are saved in the Parameters text and in kept pictures and variations.

Which picture sets can I paint from?

In Start, Pictures from picks the set the painter uses: my neon set, my soft set, my artsy set, my broken set, my nature set, my animals set, my shapes set, my pencils set, my flowers set, my abstract nature set, my abstract set, all my sets mixed, only your images, or a random combination. The sets are made from my own artworks. Your own images are mixed in with the chosen set unless you pick only your images. The same sets also supply the brushes in Paint on it.

Why does New picture not change my picture, or lose my layer, after Blend layers?

Blend layers bakes your layers into the picture and empties the layer list. The result becomes one of your images and is picked in Start, Use my image exactly. While it is picked, New picture only shows that same picture again, and the look menus are set to off by Blend, so nothing seems to change.

To get new pictures again, press Undo this in Layers (the layers come back), or set Use my image exactly to "no, paint a new picture". In that case the layer is not on the new paintings, because it was baked into the old picture.

To keep a layer on every new picture, do not press Blend layers. Leave the layer on in Layers and keep Use my image exactly on "no, paint a new picture": New picture then paints a new picture each time and puts your layer on top. To get new looks of the same blended picture, set the glitch and look menus to random (keep Menus on my image on "yes").

What are the random choices in Adjust?

Every Adjust menu now has a random choice that picks a value for you (the same seed always gives the same picture). Random combination mixes all the Adjust menus at once. Of the Adjust menus, only the Comic look is picked at random by New picture, Surprise me, Surprise looks and Variations; the others stay as you set them. Pareidolia faces are only picked at random when you tick Include faces in Surprise me. All the other menus get the same chance of being used, and the diagonal directions are one choice among the others, not several.

What does Start clean do?

It sets every menu to none or off and makes the picture black, so you can paint on it, or add glitches and signals to it, from nothing. New picture then stays clean. Untick it to get your settings back.

Why do the buttons change nothing when my own image is picked?

If you picked my image 1, exactly (Start, Use my image exactly) and Menus on my image is set to no, your image is shown untouched, so New picture, New glitch and Surprise me have nothing to change. A note above the buttons says so. Set Menus on my image to yes to let the look menus work on it, or set Use my image exactly back to no, paint a new picture. Previous IMG and Next IMG still switch between your images.

GIFs and motion

How do I make a GIF?

Make a picture you like, scroll to Make a GIF, choose your options and press Make GIF. When it is done a preview appears and Save GIF turns on. The GIF uses the menus above it, so if you changed a menu, press New picture first. Press Preview one frame to see one frame quickly before you make the whole GIF.

What does the GIF show?

It depends on Animate. With the painting being born (the default) the painting appears stroke by stroke, then fades to black and starts again (the Fade menu can fade into the empty starting canvas instead, which is the default, or cut with no fade). With the picture as it is now or one of your own images, the picture itself moves with the Motion and Signal noise you picked. Every loop is perfect: the last frame flows straight into the first.

What can I animate?

The Animate menu has three kinds of source:

  • The painting being born (the default). Motion is optional here.
  • The picture as it is now: the finished picture on screen, including anything you painted by hand. It needs a Motion or Signal noise, because otherwise nothing would move.
  • My image 1, 2, ...: an image you added. It also needs a Motion or Signal noise. With keep my image exactly ticked (the default) it gets no extra colours, glitch, texture or frame. Untick it to put the menu looks on it. Edges of my image (in Start) decides how a non-square image fits the square.
What are the motions?

shakequick small tremors
noisefilm grain, different on every frame

Use Extra motion to stack a second one, and Motion strength (gentle, medium, strong) to scale them. Every motion repeats exactly each loop, so the loop stays perfect. The slide, wander, bounce and shake motions zoom in a little so no black edges show. Colour cycle needs a modern browser; in very old ones it does nothing.

What is Signal noise?

An electronic, analog-video look that goes on top of every frame, after the Motion. Choose a strength (gentle, medium or strong) and tick the parts you want:

  • wavy rows: every row slides sideways in a wave, like a bad TV signal.
  • colour streaks: bright stripes of colour smeared sideways.
  • hot colours: very strong colours and crushed blacks.
  • static grain: fine speckle, different on every frame.
  • vertical bars: fine stripes up and down.
  • neon edges: glowing cyan, pink and white outlines.
  • sparkle: tiny bright specks and falling dotted drops.

The loop stays perfect. Signal noise changes every pixel in every frame, so the files get much bigger. For the best result use Rich (256 colours), about 400 to 500 px, 4 to 5 seconds and 10 per second, and set Keep under. It is an imitation of the look of real analog video, not the real thing.

What does Preview one frame do?

It makes one frame with your current settings (up to 600 px) and shows it in a few seconds. It does not make or save a GIF, so it is a quick way to try Signal noise, Motion and the exact-image options. Press Make GIF for the whole thing.

What does "Lock GIF settings" do?

Tick it once you have settings you like (length, size, motion, Signal noise and so on). Your choices then stay the same when you change the picture: press New picture, add an image, or open a kept or variation picture. Set Animate to the picture as it is now and every GIF you make uses the same settings on whatever picture is on screen. The lock also remembers your settings if you reload the page (in some places, like the objkt viewer, a browser may not allow that, but the lock still works until you close the page). Untick it to go back to normal.

Why does an option show a short note?

When an option does nothing at the moment, a short note under it says why, and the music options stay greyed out until you add a music file. For example: Extra motion needs a Motion, Motion strength waits for a Motion, First frame is only used for the painting being born, include my painting does not apply to your own images, and the music options wait for a music file.

How big are the files?

A plain painting GIF at 600 px is usually well under 1 MB at "Small". Motion makes files bigger because the whole picture changes every frame. As a guide, at 300 px, 4 seconds, Small: glitch jumps about 0.5 MB, colour cycle 0.8 MB, liquid wave 1.3 MB, sway 1.8 MB, spin and drift about 2 MB, noise 2.2 MB. Signal noise makes files much bigger again. For smaller files use a smaller Picture size, Small colours, 10 frames per second, a shorter Length, or set Keep under. A GIF can hold at most 256 colours.

What does "Keep under" do?

You choose a limit (1 to 15 MB). A higher limit means fewer retries, so it can be faster and keeps more resolution. Very big GIFs use more memory to play. If the first try is over it, the maker tries again with softer detail and smaller sizes until it fits. If nothing fits, it tells you it is the smallest it could make. Each retry makes the GIF again, so a tight limit takes longer.

What do the other GIF options mean?
  • Length: 4, 5, 6, 8 or 10 seconds. Long loops get a lower safe maximum picture size, and the result line tells you if it was lowered.
  • Picture size: 300 to 1200 px, always square. 600 is a good small size.
  • Smoothness: how many pictures per second, from 5 (choppy, smallest file) to 25 (very smooth, biggest file). 10 is a good small choice, 20 is smoother.
  • File size: Small, Medium or Rich (64, 128 or 256 colours).
  • First frame: only for the painting being born. The finished picture starts the GIF on the finished painting, black starts on black and is a bit smaller. The loop looks the same either way; only the first frame (the thumbnail) differs.
  • Look: clean (a bit slower) or fast (grainier).
How do I start a new GIF from scratch?

Press Clear GIF. It also clears by itself when you add or remove an image or change Animate or Motion, and when you press Make GIF, so you never save an old one by mistake.

The preview does not show but Save GIF works. Is that normal?

Some pages block previews. If the status says so, Save GIF still gives you the file.

Can I use my own GIF or video file in the GIF maker?

Yes. In the GIF maker set Animate to my own GIF or video file and pick the file with My GIF or video file (a .gif, or a video such as mp4 or webm, up to 60 MB). The page reads up to 60 frames of it at 480 px at most and plays it once across the seconds you pick. Only Signal noise and Motion are added; the look and glitch menus are not used. Tick also lay the generator picture over my file to put the picture on screen over it, half see-through. Then press Make GIF or Make video. Reading a GIF needs Edge or Chrome; if your browser cannot read a file, the page says so.

How do I make my picture black and white?

Open Adjust and pick Black and white. It starts off. The choices: soft grey (gentle, keeps some tone), grayscale (no colour at all), strong contrast (deep blacks, bright whites), black and white only (just black and white, no greys, like a stamp) and sepia (warm brown). It works on the finished picture, so it also changes a Comic look or a glitch. To flip light and dark use Reverse black and white in the same menu. Black and white is never picked by New picture, Surprise me or Variations, only by you (or by Random combination in Adjust).

What does Lock this look do? And Keep this look?

Lock this look keeps your menu settings and your picks for New picture and Surprise me, so a new seed only changes the seed. With Lock on, Variations in mode same look, new seeds keep everything exactly as you set it. In surprise looks and same seed, different looks a locked look keeps all the Glitch settings, Glow and Texture, and draws new Palette, Plates, Elements, Brush, Rarity, Layout, Background and Metal, plus a new Symmetry and Colour print.

Keep this look puts the picture in Kept pictures with every setting and both seeds. Click a kept picture to get its settings back; Save kept (ZIP) saves each picture with its settings .txt file. Clear kept removes them all, the small X removes one.

Without Lock, in same look, new seeds the menus you left on seed decides or random are drawn again for every variation, and the menus you chose yourself stay.

A picture appeared in Your images and I did not upload it. Why?

Your images only gets new pictures from you (Add images) and from two buttons that turn your work into a picture: Use my painting as image (under the painting box) and Flatten in the Layers menu. Each one joins the picture and your painting into one image and adds it to Your images as a new entry. Nothing else adds to the list by itself, and loading a kept picture or a parameters file does not. If one appeared, one of those buttons was pressed. To remove it, press Clear in Start (this clears all of your images), or untick it in the list.

Block stretch (or another look menu) does not change my picture or my painting.

Check two things. First, in Start, Menus on my image must be yes when you use one of your images; with no the image is shown untouched and the look menus (Glitch, Block stretch, texture, frame) are skipped. The line under the picture then says "exactly as it is". Second, a painting you make with the brush is a separate layer on top of the picture, so the look menus do not touch it. To put the effects on your painting, press Use my painting as image (or Flatten in Layers); the picture and painting become one image and the menus then work on all of it. Remember that the new image goes into Your images, and the result changes only after you press New glitch, New picture or Surprise me.

The Glitch menu is long. Where did Signal noise, Block stretch, Pixel stretch and Colour pass go?

Nothing was removed. In the Glitch menu these four are now one line each, with a small arrow and their main choice. Press a line to open its settings in a list beside the menu. You can open all four at the same time, and press a line again to close it. Choosing a value in a group keeps the group open, so you can change several in a row. When the window is short (or on a phone) the lists open inside the menu instead, and the menu scrolls to the end. Long choice lists, such as Background in Look, now also fit the window and scroll inside themselves.

Can I make a video as well as a GIF?

Yes. In the GIF maker press Make video, then Save video. Pick a size (480, 720 or 1080 px) and how many times it plays (once to 4 times). The video is MP4 where your browser can record it, otherwise WebM. A video with your music in it is always saved as WebM, which plays in Edge, Chrome, Firefox and VLC. A GIF and a video are both kept, so making one does not wipe the other. If your browser cannot record video, the GIF still works.

Can I add music to my GIF?

Yes. In the Music row, choose a music file (mp3, wav, ogg or m4a). The file is read inside your browser and never leaves your computer. Then pick what the music does:

  • Start music at: where in the song the GIF starts.
  • Glitch bursts on the beat: strips of the picture jump on every beat.
  • Pulse with the loudness: the picture swells with the volume.
  • Music speeds the motion: the motion gets faster on loud parts. It needs a Motion.
  • flash on the beat: a short white flash on each beat.

These wait greyed out until you add a file. Remove music clears it. The music settings are saved with Save GIF settings.

Is the GIF silent? How do I get a video with sound?

A GIF can never have sound, so Make GIF always makes a silent GIF, even with music. The beat is only in the way the picture moves, so it looks synced when you play the GIF next to the song. A GIF loops on its own length, so the beat drifts against the song after the first loop. For real sound press Make music video (the button says Make video when no music is loaded) and choose Video sound: with my music or silent. The video plays the music in real time while it records, so please wait until it is done. The result line says "with your music" or "no sound".

The beat effects look wrong or the loop jumps. Why?

Beat effects follow the song, not the loop, so the last frame does not always flow into the first. The beat finder works best with music that has a clear kick drum. Try a different Start music at or turn some effects off.

Can I save my GIF settings?

Yes. Press Save GIF settings (.txt) to keep them in a file, and Load GIF settings (.txt) to bring them back. The GIF settings are also part of the Parameters text under the picture. Lock GIF settings keeps your choices when you change the picture.

Menus

Some menus are greyed out. Is something broken?

No. A greyed menu or option would do nothing with your current choices, and a short note under it says why. Change the setting it depends on and it turns back on.

Why does Plates not do anything?

The Collage layouts build their own patchwork, so Plates is not used there. Also, with only one or two pictures (your own images only), the plate styles that need more pictures are greyed out.

Why can I not pick a Metal?

Metal is for legendary pictures. It works when Palette is "seed decides" or "original" and Rarity is "legendary" or "seed decides". With a fixed palette or a lower rarity it is greyed out. A metal makes the picture legendary.

Why is Rarity greyed out?

Rarity steers the palette and the elements when you leave them on "seed decides". If you chose both yourself, there is nothing left for Rarity to decide.

What are the extra glitch styles for?

You can stack up to three glitch styles: the main one and Extra glitch style 2 and 3. They work with any main style, including none, mix, geometric and random. Glitch amount needs at least one glitch style.

Four styles are new: scan tear (bands of the picture torn sideways with a bright edge), ghost echo (faded copies trailing off in one direction), datamosh drag (blocks dragged into streaks) and CRT interlace (every other line shifted, tinted and darker). They only appear when you pick them, and the random combination does not use them.

What are the Fibonacci options?

Three menu choices use the Fibonacci / golden ratio. They are only used when you pick them yourself. Random combination and Surprise me never choose them, so pictures you made before still come back exactly the same.

  • Layout, Fibonacci spiral (golden angle): the pieces are placed along a golden-angle spiral, the way seeds sit in a sunflower. How much you see depends on the picture, because big strokes can cover it. It is clearest with brushes like picture stamp.
  • Plates, golden tiles (Fibonacci rectangles): the picture is cut into tiles that each get 61.8% of what is left, and each tile uses a different plate. It needs at least 3 plates, otherwise it becomes a blend.
  • Texture, sunflower dots (golden angle): your picture is rebuilt from about 2,600 round dots on a sunflower spiral. Bright parts get bigger dots.
What is the soft cross stitch fabric texture?

In Touches, the Texture menu has soft cross stitch fabric. It turns the whole picture into a grid of 130 by 130 fine X stitches, each one in the colour of that spot, on a soft cloth. It is a strong effect, because it also reduces the picture to that grid, so fine detail is lost. It works best on pictures with clear shapes and colours. It is not used by the random combination or by Surprise me, so you pick it yourself. It also works on your own image when Menus on my image is on.

What is Signal noise on a still picture?

In Glitch, the Signal noise menu gives the picture an electronic, analog-video look: wavy rows, bright colour streaks, very strong colours, grain, vertical bars, neon edges and sparkle. Pick a strength (gentle, medium, strong) and a Signal look (mix, wavy TV, neon streaks, hot VHS, glitter, everything or random). Signal look only works when Signal noise is on, and with it off the menu says so. It goes on after the glitch styles. It works on painted pictures and also on your own image shown exactly: pick a strength and it is applied, whatever Menus on my image says. It is an imitation of the look of real analog video, not the real thing.

Can the picture be mirrored diagonally?

Yes. Symmetry (in Touches) now has mirror diagonal in both directions, both diagonals together (X) and an 8-way mirror, besides the left-right, up-down and 4-way mirrors and the kaleidoscopes.

What is Pareidolia (faces)?

A menu in Touches, right under Symmetry. It makes the picture read as a face, a skull, a mask, a creature, a dragon, a cat, a wolf, an owl, an alien, a robot, a demon, a ghost, a totem of three heads or a wall of repeated faces. Nothing is drawn: no eyes, nose or mouth are added. It only shapes the head, hides a soft mirror seam, and puts dark hollows and light patches where a face would have them, so your eye finds the face by itself.

It is none until you pick a style. Pick random combination to let the seed choose one or two styles for you.

Under the style menu there is a box Include faces in Surprise me. It is off by default, and then Surprise me never turns a face on or changes the one you chose. Tick it and every Surprise me (and every Variation in the "looks" mode) picks a random face style with the strong Face look. Lock this look keeps your face even when the box is ticked.

What does Face look do?

It is the first choice inside Pareidolia settings and sets several values at once. Strong (the main style, and what you get when you first pick a style) makes the hollows and lights deep so the face stands out. Clear is in between. Gentle is soft and more abstract. You can still change any single setting afterwards; the Face look stays as it is.

What do the other Pareidolia settings do?
  • Mix with a second style blends two styles; Mix between the two styles says how much of each.
  • Abstraction melts and swirls the shapes, up to "barely a face".
  • Strength is how much of the effect shows. "Off" gives your plain picture.
  • Applied is when it happens: before the glitch (the glitch can break the face up), after the glitch, after Touches, or all the way through (slower).
  • Mirror copies the left half, the right half or the brighter half. Centre line and Seam softness move the mirror and hide its line.
  • Head shape, Ears and horns, Head size (up to "fills the whole picture") and Head position.
  • Light and dark map and Depth set how much the head stands out and how dark the hollows go.
  • Variation makes the shape different on every seed, or always the same.

Some of these are greyed out with a short note when they cannot do anything, for example the mix is off until you pick a second style, and Centre line and Seam softness are off when Mirror is "no mirror".

Is my Pareidolia choice saved? Does it make the page slower?

Yes, it is saved. It is a line in the Parameters text (Copy, Save .txt, Apply), in Keep this look, and it is used for every picture in Variations, in the ZIP, and in the GIF frames. New picture, New glitch and Surprise me keep it. Clear parameters puts it back to none.

A face takes extra work for every picture, about a second more on a normal computer and longer on an old one. The page shows that it is working. If it feels slow, pick before the glitch or turn the face off while you try other settings.

Why does my ink or watercolor picture have a paper background?

Ink, calligraphy, watercolor and pencil brushes paint on paper unless you pick a background yourself. A note under Background tells you.

What does Paint on it do?

You can paint on the picture with brushes cut from the pictures in your set. Choose a colour mode, size and opacity, then drag on the picture. Undo and Clear paint turn on once you have painted. Your painting is included when you save and, if you like, in GIFs and variations.

What are Variations?

Press Make variations for 6, 12 or 24 versions, or "fill empty spots" to refill gaps left by the small X on a variation. Mode decides what changes:

  • same look, new seeds: keeps your menus and only changes the seeds. If your look has nothing random in it (for example only a colour change), the variations will look the same, and the page says so.
  • surprise looks: every variation gets a new look drawn from ALL menus, old and new: the glitch styles, Adjust, Comic look, Plaid, the Glitch fine settings, the stretch settings and, now and then, Pareidolia (faces). Each item is only sometimes on, so the variations stay different but not too busy.
  • same seed, different looks: keeps the seed and draws the looks from all menus in the same way.

My images in variations is greyed out until you have added 2 or more images, picked one in Use my image exactly (Start) and set Menus on my image to yes. Then you can choose "next of my images each time". The painting options turn on after you have painted something. Click a variation to open it big, press Stop to stop early, or save them all as a ZIP.

What is Line direction?

In Glitch, Line direction decides which way the line glitches and Signal noise run: off, horizontal (as before), vertical, horizontal and vertical together, diagonal up, diagonal down, random mix (a mix of all) or random single (one direction, picked for you). It is greyed out until a line style or Signal noise is on. The older menu Glitch line direction (sideways, up and down, diagonal, crossed) is still there for the first group of line glitches.

What are Rain and Code rain?

New glitch styles (at the end of the Glitch style lists): rain (white), rain (rainbow), code rain 0/1 (white) and code rain 0/1 (rainbow). They are thin, scarce and discreet. "Rain and code amount" makes them rare or a few more. "Rain and code colours" is auto by default: light on dark parts, with a dark edge on light parts so you can still see them.

What are the Water ripples?

Two new glitch styles. "Concentric" are rings spreading from a few points. "Lake, see-through" is vertical, like a reflection in a lake. Neither adds a colour tint, they only bend and light the picture.

What is the Colorama look?

In Touches. It turns the light and dark of the picture into neon rainbow bands. Choose whole picture or patches only, how much stays untouched (nothing, some, most) and the strength. There is also a Colorama palette in the Palette menu.

What are the new Signal looks?

Three new entries in Signal look: colorama patches, untouched zones (some boxes of the picture stay clean) and vertical hold bars (rolling TV bars). Signal strength is the Signal noise menu: none, gentle, medium, strong.

What do New picture, Surprise me and Variations use?

Surprise me works as before: it picks the Start, Look, Touches and Glitch menus, and it only uses the newest looks (Block stretch, Pixel stretch, Colour pass, the new dusty glitches, Rain, Code rain, Water ripples, Line directions, Colorama) if you tick Include new looks in Surprise me (off by default).

New picture and Variations (surprise looks, same seed different looks) always draw from everything we have, except Frames, which stay off: Adjust (Comic look too), Look, Touches, Glitch, Signals, Plaid, the stretch menus, background, metal and now and then Pareidolia. In New picture, a menu you changed yourself stays as you set it, and Lock this look keeps everything and only changes the seed.

If you use one of your own images shown exactly as it is (Menus on my image = no), nothing can change it, so these buttons change nothing.

Do the new looks move in a GIF?

Yes. Rain falls and wraps around, code columns drop and flicker, ripples spread, the Colorama look turns its colours round and loops. In Animate, Signal noise has three more parts: untouched zones, vertical hold bars and colorama patches.

What does "fill empty spots" do?

Every variation has a small X in its corner. Press it to remove that variation and leave an empty spot. Choose "fill empty spots" in the Variations count menu and press Make variations to paint new ones only in the empty spots.

What is "same seed, different looks"?

A Variations mode. It keeps the seed and changes the menus, so you see what the same seed looks like with different settings.

Does Surprise me use Frame and Pixelate?

No. Surprise me and the surprise Variations modes never pick a Frame or Pixelate: both are set to none. Add them by hand if you want them. A locked look keeps whatever you set.

What is in the Adjust menu? What is the Comic look?

Adjust has the Comic look and simple corrections: Contrast enhancement, Deepen, Brighten, Darken, Decolor, Saturation, Backlight, Fill flash and Reverse black and white. All are off until you pick a strength.

The Comic look gives the picture ink outlines. Choose soft ink or bold ink, with bright or flat colours, or the bright poster look, or a black and white comic. It also works on your own image.

What do very soft, light, medium and strong mean?

Most glitch menus now have steps from very soft to strong (for example Glitch amount, Signal noise, Pixel stretch, Block stretch see-through, Colour pass strength, Rain and code amount). "Very soft" is the lightest, for when a look is too aggressive. Old choices are unchanged, so old seeds look the same as before.

What is Block stretch?

Block stretch smears the picture into long lines inside random blocks. In Glitch choose how many blocks (very few to many). The other menus are greyed out until you do:

  • Stretch lines: thin to very thick (1 to 12 px).
  • Stretch direction: mixed, vertical or horizontal.
  • Stretch colour: a colourful line of the picture, random or mixed.
  • Stretch see-through: solid, bold, medium, soft, very soft, see-through (the picture shows through) or mixed. "off" draws nothing.
  • Stretch applied: after the glitch, before the glitch, or at the very end.
  • Stretch pale lines in empty areas: very pale grey lines on black, white or empty spots; "opposite colour" uses the opposite colour instead.

It is off by default.

What is Pixel stretch?

Pixel stretch pulls the pixels into thin streaks. Pixel stretch sets the amount: off, very soft, 1 light, 2 medium, 3 strong, 4 very strong. Pixel stretch direction picks diagonal (down right), diagonal (up right), both diagonals, vertical (column smear), horizontal or mixed. Pixel stretch applied says whether it goes before the glitch, after it, or at the very end.

Three more menus, Vertical, Horizontal and Diagonal pixel stretch (scarce, medium, strong), add that kind of line on top, on their own or together, so you can mix horizontal and vertical.

What is Colour pass?

Colour pass lays a colour look over the whole picture. Choose one: rainbow, black and white, sepia, blue print, warm, cool, sunset, teal and orange, night vision, heat map, negative, pick a colour (then choose it in Colour pass colour), random colour, one of 7 neon pairs (for example neon green and orange, or neon pink and cyan), or a random neon pair. Colour pass strength goes from very soft to full. For Negative, medium is a true photo negative and strong adds contrast. Colour pass applied says before the glitch, after it or at the very end. It is off by default.

What are the dusty glitch styles?

The old dusty noise mixes boxes of grain with speckles. There are now separate choices: dusty boxes and dusty speckles, each in black and white or in colour, and dusty random (it picks one of the four). They are in all three glitch menus, so you can combine them with each other and with other styles.

What do Glitch see-through, Glitch look and Glitch line direction do?

Glitch see-through makes the glitch more transparent, in 10 steps from solid to "barely there". Glitch look sets how thick the lines are (normal, medium, thinner, hairlines). Glitch line direction sets the first group of line glitches: sideways, up and down, diagonal or crossed.

What is the plaid texture?

In Texture you can pick a plaid: in the dark spaces, a big tartan in the dark spaces, soft plaid over everything, or only plaid (a plaid picture to glitch). Plaid applied says whether it goes after the glitch or before it (so the glitch works on the plaid). Plaid colours and Plaid squares change the colours and the size. They are greyed out until you pick a plaid.

Your own images

Can I use my image exactly as it is, without changing it?

Yes. After you add an image, open the menu Use my image exactly (in Start) and pick it. The picture then shows your image untouched: no painting, no colours, no glitch styles, no texture, no frame. The one exception is Signal noise (Glitch): pick a strength and it goes on your image. You can still paint on it and save it. Edges of my image chooses between cut to fill the picture and keep the whole image (with black bars). New picture, New glitch, Surprise me and the look menus (except Signal noise) will not change it until you set the menu back to no, paint a new picture. Pictures are shown up to 2000 px.

In the GIF maker, choose Animate → my image and keep keep my image exactly ticked. The GIF then moves your original image with no extra look added. A GIF can hold only 256 colours, so choose Rich (256 colours) for the closest match.

Can I go back to the previous picture? (Previous IMG and Next IMG)

Yes. Next to Undo and Redo are two buttons, Previous IMG and Next IMG.

  • With no images of your own added: each time you press New picture, New glitch, Surprise me or Clear parameters, the picture you were looking at is remembered (the last 30). Previous IMG brings it back, Next IMG moves forward again.
  • With 2 or more of your own images added: they step through your images, one by one, and go round at the ends. Your image is shown with the look settings you have.

Your painting is not part of this: it stays on the picture. To keep a picture for good, press Keep this look.

How do I glitch my own painting?

Paint on the picture (or tick Blank canvas to paint on black). Then press Use my painting as image, next to Clear paint. Your painting, together with the picture under it, becomes one ordinary image in your list, my image with the next number. The paint layer is emptied, so nothing is left behind.

Now it is like any image of yours: Menus on my image is switched to yes for you, so symmetry, glitch styles, Signal noise, texture and frame all work on it. Use New glitch or Surprise me for new looks on it. Make variations does not use it: variations paint new pictures, and they can carry your painting if Include my painting is ticked. You can paint on it again and repeat. Blank canvas is switched off after flattening.

What does Menus on my image do?

When you use your image exactly, it stays untouched by default, except for Signal noise, which always goes on when you pick a strength. Set Menus on my image to "yes" and the other look menus (glitch styles, pixelate, symmetry, colour print, light, texture, frame) are applied too, so you can iterate on it. With "no" those menus do not change it, and a short note says so.

Can I use my own pictures?

Yes, up to 12. Use Add images. They stay in your browser and are never uploaded anywhere. For painting I use small copies of them (to show one untouched, see the next question). They are mixed in with the set you chose; pick "only your images" to use just those.

"Only your images" and "my collage" are greyed out.

They need at least one image of yours. Add one and they turn on. If you clear your images while they are selected, the app goes back to a normal set and the normal collage.

Can I make a GIF from one of my images?

Yes. Add the image, and the Animate menu selects it for you. Pick a Motion or Signal noise and press Make GIF. Without either, nothing would move, so the maker asks you to choose one.

What are the collage layouts and the Collage style menu?

In Look, Layout sets how the paint is placed: the seed decides, a random combination, or one of cloud, swirl, rings, grid, vortex arms, centre burst, diagonal band, mirror, squares, X, abstract vitro, Fibonacci spiral, and the newer river, corners, islands, mountains, dome, curtain, field, city, strands, beams, bands, pillars, tufts and ribbons.

Three of the choices are collages. collage is a patchwork painted from the sets. collage of my pictures and collage of my pictures as tiles use your own pictures (add them first, and tick the ones to use). The tiles version shows your pictures as tiles, with nothing painted over them.

Collage style picks how the pieces are cut and joined: hard-edged tiles, soft blends, strips, bubbles, marbled liquid, smoke clouds, double exposure, diagonal fades, honeycomb, torn edges, zigzag cuts, paper pile and more, or a random style each time. Paint strength on collage says how much paint covers the pictures (light, medium, heavy).

Saving

How do I save a picture?

Save PNG saves the picture, including your painting. If your browser blocks it, right-click the picture and choose Save image as.

What does Keep this look do?

It puts the picture in a row of up to 24 kept pictures. Save kept (ZIP) downloads them all with a text file of settings for each. Clear kept empties the row. The buttons are greyed out until there is something to act on.

How do I get the same picture back later?

Open Parameters of this picture, press Save .txt or Copy. Later, paste the list into the box (or Load .txt file) and press Apply. All menus are saved, the new ones too, and so are your GIF settings. If the list uses your own images and you have not added them, the app falls back to a normal set and tells you what it skipped. The list describes the picture above, so press New picture or New glitch first if you changed menus and want the list to show them.

Does "Clear parameters" delete my pictures?

No. It puts every menu back to its default and picks a new seed.
